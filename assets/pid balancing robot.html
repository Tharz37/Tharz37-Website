The "Black Box" Challenge

Every robotics student has seen a PID balancing bot. It’s a classic project. However, we chose to use the Indigenous ARIES V3 Board instead of a standard Arduino. This decision turned a simple project into a complex research task.

Since the board was new, there were no libraries for the MPU-6050 gyroscope. Our bot was effectively blind.

Custom Architecture

My teammate, Shahazad Abdulla, engineered a custom library from scratch by digging into datasheets and I2C protocols. This breakthrough allowed us to open the "black box" and feed clean data into our PID algorithm.

Mission Success

Watching the bot flicker, fight gravity, and finally stand stable on its own was a victory. We didn't just build a balancer; we built the driver software to make it possible.
